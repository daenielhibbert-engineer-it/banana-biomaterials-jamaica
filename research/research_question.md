# BananaLoop: Research Question and Study Framework

## Working Title

**BananaLoop: Optimising Banana Pseudostem Fibre-Reinforced Cassava Starch Films Through Experimental Characterisation and Computational Modelling**

## Background

Banana and plantain pseudostems are agricultural residues generated during harvesting. This project investigates whether fibres recovered from this biomass can reinforce cassava-starch-based films, potentially creating a higher-value use for agricultural waste in Jamaica.

## Research Question

How do pseudostem fibre loading and glycerol concentration influence the mechanical properties and water response of cassava-starch-based biodegradable composite films?

## Aim

To investigate the effects of pseudostem fibre loading and glycerol concentration on the properties of cassava-starch films, then develop a computational model to analyse and identify promising formulations.

## Objectives

1. Review existing research on banana pseudostem fibres, starch-based films and biodegradable composites.
2. Prepare pseudostem fibre and develop a repeatable film-formulation procedure under teacher supervision.
3. Investigate how changes in fibre loading and glycerol concentration affect selected film properties.
4. Analyse experimental measurements using Python.
5. Develop and evaluate a mathematical model relating formulation variables to measured properties.
6. Identify a promising formulation based on the measured property trade-offs.

## Working Hypothesis

Increasing pseudostem fibre content may improve the film's resistance to applied force up to an appropriate concentration, while glycerol concentration may influence flexibility and water response. The effects are expected to depend on the interaction between formulation variables.

## Scope

The investigation will focus on formulation, mechanical behaviour, water response and computational analysis. It will not claim commercial food-packaging suitability, food safety or environmental superiority without appropriate testing.
